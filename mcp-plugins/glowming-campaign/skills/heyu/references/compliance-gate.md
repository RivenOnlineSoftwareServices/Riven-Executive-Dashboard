# Compliance gate: Glowming customer copy

Every new or changed customer-facing line passes this gate before Heyu offers it as final. "Customer-facing" means anything a customer, affiliate, host or member of the public will read: advert text, headlines, picture text, captions, hashtags, emails, WhatsApp messages, website and Journey page wording.

The gate is a careful self-check, not legal advice. It applies on every surface: adverts, posts, website, emails, print and in person. There is no surface where looser health wording is allowed.

**Anton's approval overrides the health-claim rules in this file** (Riaan's standing rule, 1 Oct 2026: "Yes it actually does override when coming from Anton"). Heyu never PROPOSES a banned or unsourced claim. When Anton asks for one, or writes one himself: say the risk once, in one plain sentence (for example "Fat-burning and craving claims can draw an advertising complaint and get adverts or emails rejected"), show him the exact words, and ask "Do you approve these exact words?". Only on his clear yes, append to `anton.md`: `CLAIM APPROVED | <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM SAST> | "<the exact sentence>"` (one whole sentence of three words or more per line; several sentences may share a line), then use those words exactly as approved, as their own sentence, never extended or combined with other claims. The guard allows a claim word only inside a whole sentence recorded this way, and also checks other health, benefit and medical wording (energy, gut, digestion, immunity, bloating, prevents, treats and the like) the same way. The owner-approved live line "Gut health, energy, immunity and skin glow" needs no new approval.

**Still never overridden, by anyone:** tracking links, the never-do actions (sending, publishing, changing adverts), the campaign skill's checked edit pathway and company-file protections, and the website (never changed from here). Outside those, Heyu disagrees once with a reason and then follows Anton.

## Where it bites

| Surface | Who stops it |
|---|---|

| TikTok, organic Instagram and Facebook posts | Platform flags and reach limits |

The same rules apply to all of them.

## The rules

### 1. No new health claims without a written source
- Heyu never writes a NEW health or benefit claim unless a written source for it exists. If Anton asks for one, say plainly: "I can't write a new health claim without a written source. Here's a version built on taste, routine and the ingredients." Then give it.
- No promise that Glowming cures, treats, prevents or heals anything.
- No weight-loss, fat-burning, "lose X kg", detox, bloating-cure, appetite or craving claims, anywhere ("beach body", "bikini body", "lose weight", "anti-ageing", "appetite suppressant", "fewer cravings", "detox" are all out).
- No timed promise ("results in 7 days", "within 7-10 days", "by week 3 your skin will...").
- No "clinically proven", "doctor recommended", "medical grade", "guaranteed results".
- No before-and-after pictures or wording.
- No medicine or drug names.

**Owner-approved wording that is already live** may be reused, under these conditions: word for word, never extended, never paraphrased into a stronger claim, never combined into a new claim, and marked **"owner-approved, live; source owed"** where no written source exists. See the list at the end of this file.

**Not health claims (safe to write fresh):** ingredient names as listed in `voice-and-wording.md`, flavours, taste, the routine ("make it in the morning and sip it through your day"), the Glowming Ritual, the Journey and its check-ins, the offer, the prize, "Holiday-ready, all year."

### 2. Do not tell the reader what is wrong with their body (Meta personal-attribute rule)
Meta rejects adverts that assert or imply a personal characteristic of the viewer: their health, body, weight or condition.
- **REVISE:** "Your body has been asking for this", "Glowming is what your body has been looking for" (both banned by the owners), "Tired of your bloated stomach?", "Your skin needs this", "Feeling bloated?", "You're always tired".
- New lines never ask about or describe the reader's body or symptoms. Talk about Glowming, the ritual or the offer instead.
- The two approved I1 picture headlines about bloating stay where they are already approved, with a risk note (see the list at the end). They are NOT a model for new adverts.
- "You" and "your" are fine for actions and choices: "your favourite flavour", "your 6-week Glowming Journey", "your daily Glowming Ritual", "your pouches arrive every month".

### 3. No competitor disparagement
- Never name or picture a competitor in customer copy, and never claim to be better than a named brand.
- Category-level framing is fine when it is true and not a put-down: "a wellness drink you mix from a sachet", "one easy daily habit". Say what Glowming is, not what others are not.
- Never mention Dis-Chem.

### 4. Disclosure: #ad on affiliate and host content
- Any message, post or story an affiliate or host shares that carries her code or link must say so: "#ad" plus "I earn commission" (for example "#ad I earn commission on orders with my code." or "#ad (Full disclosure: I earn a commission when you order with my code.)").
- Do not word a discount so it reads as free ("Try Glowming on me" was replaced for this reason).
- Host partnership adverts run on the host's own post and her own tracked link; Heyu does not write the host's own words.

### 5. Competition and offer wording
- Anything about the prize carries "T&Cs apply." If approved copy about the prize lacks it, add it and mark it "(compliance addition)".
- The prize is "Win Cape Town for two". Entries close at 23:59 on Monday 30 November. State entry routes only as listed in `campaign-rules.md`.
- One discount per order: R55 Welcome and the R999 Journey price do not combine. Say so when both appear together.
- No "normal" or crossed-out price unless it is approved with a source. "Normally R1,157" stays out of paid adverts until a written source exists.
- Subscription saving: rand only, never a percentage, everywhere except the approved launch email (see `voice-and-wording.md`).
- "Free to join" / "Free to enter" are true for the Journey and the competition.

### 6. Honesty
- Customer quotes must be real, quoted word for word, first name only, and already approved. New quotes need Anton's OK and the customer's message on record. A quote is never used to say something Glowming could not say itself in a new line.
- Never present an owner's, staff member's, affiliate's or host's own review as a customer review.
- Never invent facts, numbers, results, prize details or dates. Never invent anything about the person receiving a message.
- No fake urgency (a deadline that is not real).

### 7. Data and consent
- Copy must not ask for ID numbers, medical history, banking details or other sensitive personal data. Sign-up and diary wording is fixed by the Journey app; do not rewrite consent wording.

### 8. Language and punctuation (customer copy rules from the owners)
- English only.
- No em dashes (the long dash). Use a full stop, comma or colon.
- Glowming's own spellings and terms as in `voice-and-wording.md` ("Halaal", "the Glowming Journey", "Glowming Ritual").

## How to run the gate

1. Split the copy into separate lines: headline, main text, short line, button, picture text, hashtags, subject, preview.
2. Check each line against rules 1 to 8 and the banned list in `voice-and-wording.md`.
3. A line is **PASS** only if no rule is broken, or it is an exact, unchanged line from the owner-approved list used where it is approved. Anything else is **REVISE**, whoever asked for it.
4. For every REVISE, write a compliant alternative that keeps the job of the line (same offer, same call to action, similar length), then check the alternative too.
5. The overall result is PASS only when every line is PASS.
