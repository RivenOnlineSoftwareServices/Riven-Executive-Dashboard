# Compliance gate: Glowming customer copy

Every new or changed customer-facing line passes this gate before Heyu offers it as final. "Customer-facing" means anything a customer, affiliate, host or member of the public will read: advert text, headlines, picture text, captions, hashtags, emails, WhatsApp messages, website and Journey page wording.

The gate is a careful self-check, not legal advice. Anton and the owners make the final call; a line the owners have ruled on is not argued again.

## Which rules bite where

| Surface | Who actually stops it | How strict |
|---|---|---|
| Meta adverts (Facebook, Instagram), boosted posts | Meta's ad review, which also reads the page the advert links to | Strictest |
| TikTok, organic Instagram and Facebook posts | Platform flags and reach limits | Strict |
| Website, Journey page, emails to customers | Meta reviews the pages adverts link to; email is read as advertising | Strict |
| Emails to businesses, print, in person (flyers, posters, counter cards, events) | South African advertising rules (ARB), rarely enforced on print | Owners allow bolder wording (see rule 9) |

Whatever the surface, the honesty rules (rule 6) always hold.

## The rules

### 1. No unsupported health claims
- No promise that Glowming cures, treats, prevents or heals anything.
- No detox results, weight-loss, fat-burning or "lose X kg" promises online.
- No bloating cure or timed promise ("no more bloating", "results in 7 days", "within 7-10 days", "by week 3 your skin will...").
- No "clinically proven", "doctor recommended", "medical grade", "guaranteed results".
- No before-and-after pictures or wording.
- No medicine or drug names (for example GLP-1 injections) in paid adverts.

**Allowed benefit wording** (already in approved or live Glowming copy):
- "Gut health, energy, immunity and skin glow" and the benefit bubble "Gut health · energy · immunity · skin glow" (approved for this campaign; see owner rulings).
- "Supports an active lifestyle" and "Gut cleansing" (live on the Glowming homepage; the owners ruled to keep "gut cleansing").
- "Green tea + L-carnitine for your active lifestyle", "Marine collagen + glutathione for your glow", "Psyllium, chia + okra for easy digestion" (live Journey page wording). Milky Matcha has no psyllium (its fibre is from okra and chitosan), so do not use the psyllium line about Milky Matcha.
- "Fewer cravings" (online). "Appetite suppressant" is print and in-person only.
- "Holiday-ready, all year."
- Ingredient facts as listed in `voice-and-wording.md`.
- Real customer words quoted exactly from the approved list (rule 6).

Safe directions when rewriting: taste and flavour, the daily Glowming Ritual, the routine of making it in the morning and sipping through the day, the ingredients by name, the Journey and its check-ins, the offer, the prize.

### 2. Do not tell the reader what is wrong with their body (Meta personal-attribute rule)
Meta rejects adverts that assert or imply a personal characteristic of the viewer: their health, body, weight or condition.
- **REVISE:** "Your body has been asking for this", "Glowming is what your body has been looking for" (both banned by the owners), "Tired of your bloated stomach?", "Your skin needs this", "You're always tired".
- **Owner ruling (28 Sep):** a question about how someone feels is acceptable, statements about them are not. The approved I1 headlines "Bloating, poor digestion or low energy?" and "Feeling bloated, backed-up or low energy?" stay as approved. For new lines, prefer to talk about Glowming, the ritual or the offer rather than the reader's body.
- "You" and "your" are fine for actions and choices: "your favourite flavour", "your 6-week Glowming Journey", "your daily Glowming Ritual", "your pouches arrive every month".

### 3. No competitor disparagement
- Never name or picture a competitor in customer copy, and never claim to be better than a named brand.
- Category-level framing is fine when it is true and not a put-down: "a wellness drink you mix from a sachet", "one easy daily habit". Say what Glowming is, not what others are not.
- Never mention Dis-Chem.

### 4. Disclosure: #ad on affiliate and host content
- Any message, post or story an affiliate or host shares that carries her code or link must say so: "#ad" plus "I earn commission" (for example "#ad I earn commission on orders with my code." or "(Full disclosure: I earn a commission when you order with my code.)").
- Do not word a discount so it reads as free ("Try Glowming on me" was replaced for this reason).
- Host partnership adverts run on the host's own post and her tracked link; Heyu does not write the host's own words.

### 5. Competition and offer wording
- Anything about the prize carries "T&Cs apply".
- The prize is "Win Cape Town for two". Entries close at 23:59 on Monday 30 November. State entry routes only as listed in `campaign-rules.md`.
- One discount per order: R55 Welcome and the R999 Journey price do not combine. Say so when both appear together.
- Do not state a "normal" or crossed-out price unless it is approved. "Normally R1,157" stays OUT of paid adverts until a written source exists.
- "Over 70 million drinks sold globally" is allowed, including in paid adverts (owner ruling 28 Sep). Always "drinks", never "sachets".
- "Free to join" / "Free to enter" are true for the Journey and the competition.

### 6. Honesty (always, every surface)
- Customer quotes must be real, quoted word for word, first name only, and already approved. New quotes need Anton's OK and the customer's message on record.
- Never present an owner's, staff member's, affiliate's or host's own review as a customer review.
- Never invent facts, numbers, results, prize details or dates. Never invent anything about the person receiving a message.
- No fake urgency (a deadline that is not real).

### 7. Data and consent
- Copy must not ask for ID numbers, medical history, banking details or other sensitive personal data. Sign-up and diary wording is fixed by the Journey app; do not rewrite consent wording.

### 8. Language and punctuation (customer copy rules from the owners)
- English only.
- No em dashes (the long dash). Use a full stop, comma or colon.
- Glowming's own spellings and terms as in `voice-and-wording.md` ("Halaal", "the Glowming Journey", "Glowming Ritual").

### 9. Print, in-person and business emails
- The owners allow bolder wording here: "beach body", "bikini", "lose weight", "anti-ageing", "appetite suppressant", and Anton's full benefits line in emails to businesses.
- Still no cure claims, no timed results and no invented facts.
- These words never move into Meta, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or the website.

## How to run the gate

1. Split the copy into separate lines: headline, main text, short line, button, picture text, hashtags, subject, preview.
2. Check each line against rules 1 to 8 (and 9 for print or business emails), and against the banned list in `voice-and-wording.md`.
3. A line is **PASS** only if no rule is broken, or it is an owner ruling above. Otherwise it is **REVISE**.
4. For every REVISE, write a compliant alternative that keeps the job of the line (same offer, same call to action, similar length), then check the alternative too.
5. The overall result is PASS only when every line is PASS.


After the table, at most two lines: the most important fix, and what Anton does next. Keep reasons in plain words, never rule numbers alone.
