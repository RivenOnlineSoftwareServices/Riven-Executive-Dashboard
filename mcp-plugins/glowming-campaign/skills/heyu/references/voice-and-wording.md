# Glowming voice and wording

## The Glowming voice

Taken from the approved campaign copy (see `campaign-wording.md`):

- **Warm and everyday.** Talks like a friend who drinks it: "Mix a sachet, sip it through your day, and find your favourite of our four flavours."
- **Simple and concrete.** Short sentences. Names the real thing: the sachet, the flavour, the ingredient, the day, the price.
- **Inviting, never pushy.** "Join the Glowming Journey free", "Your Glowming Ritual is waiting." No shouting, no guilt, no fear.
- **Offer stated plainly.** "Any 3 flavours, R999, delivered." Numbers written the way people say them.
- **Signed by the brand.** "The Glowming team". Never a person's name unless Anton asks for a personal note in his own name.
- **South African English.** colour, flavour, fibre, programme, energised (keep a customer's own spelling when quoting her).
- **Light emoji only where the platform is personal** (WhatsApp, Instagram stories, affiliate share messages): one or two, never in Meta advert headlines.

## Banned words and phrases (search every draft before offering it)

- "tub" / "tubs" for Glowming.
- "Your body has been asking for this" and "Glowming is what your body has been looking for" (owner ban, 29 Sep).
- "Normally R1,157" in paid adverts (no written source yet).
- Any statement or question about the reader's body, skin, symptoms or condition in new copy (see `compliance-gate.md`, rule 2).
- Everywhere, print and in person included: "detox", "weight loss", "lose weight", "fat burning", "burn fat", "metabolism", "beach body", "bikini body", "summer body", "anti-ageing", "appetite suppressant", "fewer cravings", "cures", "heals", "treats", "clinically proven", "guaranteed".
- Any new health or benefit claim without a written source.
- Any evening, night, before-bed or wind-down framing, for any flavour.
- "one sachet a day" as a rule (say "one Glowming Ritual a day"; people may sip a sachet over one or two days).
- Afrikaans, or any single language other than English. Glowming is English only, everywhere it produces anything. Do not offer translations.
- Dis-Chem, in any role.
- Competitor names.
- Em dashes.

This list is what Heyu flags. When an owner asks for a banned word or line anyway, warn once and write it (Riaan, 2026-10-02).

- **Glowming** is a wellness drink you mix from a sachet and sip through your day.
- **Four flavours:** Pomegranate, Café Latte, Choco and Milky Matcha (write Café with the accent).
- **Pack:** pouches of 10 sachets. One sachet is one drink.
- **The 6-week Glowming Journey** uses 3 pouches (30 sachets).
- **Ingredients as approved:** "marine collagen, glutathione, L-carnitine, moringa, fibre and green tea extract and much more" (I1 wording), or "marine collagen, moringa and fibre in every sachet" (I3 wording). Ingredient count: "14 ingredients". No per-sachet doses are published, so never quote amounts.
- **Flavour-specific care:** Milky Matcha's fibre is from okra and chitosan (no psyllium). Marine collagen is fish-derived, so allergy questions go to the product label.
- **Benefit wording:** only the owner-approved, live lines in `compliance-gate.md`, word for word, never extended.
- **How it is used:** make it in the morning and sip it through your day. Never position it for the evening.
- **Social proof:** "Rated 4.5 out of 5 in 141 reviews" (as approved for I2; check the current figure before reusing it in new copy). "Over 70 million drinks sold globally" (owner-approved, live; source owed; reuse word for word).
- **First-use advice:** there is no approved first-use line. An older fixed sentence ends "This eases your body into the detox gently."; do NOT use it (it is a detox claim about the reader's body). If Anton needs first-use advice, offer a neutral line such as "First time? ## Tracking links (for caption.txt and any advert link)

This section is for READING links, so Heyu can explain them. Heyu never makes, edits or "corrects" a link.

- **Existing links and `utm_` values are never changed**, whatever the recipe below says and whoever asks. Copy them exactly.
- **New links are made by Riaan's side only.** If one is needed, request it under `## Questions` in `anton.md` (append only, through the campaign skill).

How the links are built (Riaan, 26 Sep), for reference:

```
?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=<paid_social|organic_social>&utm_campaign=glowming-journey-2026&utm_content=<code>&utm_term=<placement>
```

- `utm_medium`: `paid_social` for paid adverts, `organic_social` for organic posts.
- `utm_content`: the advert code plus design letter, lower case, for example `a2-b` (a = photo design, b = colour design). This is how each advert's orders are counted. A copy of an advert gets its own code.
- `utm_term`: `feed`, `story` or `reel`.
- **Host partnership adverts (I4, A9, A10) keep the host's own tracked link**, with the tags already added. Never replace it with a Glowming page link.
- Landing pages for new links (Riaan's side decides): A2 and A7 (the R999 offer) go to the shop, `https://glowming.co.za/collections/all`; other Glowming adverts to `https://glowming.co.za/pages/journey`. This never applies to an existing link or to the host adverts.
- Meta advert names follow `GJ26 | <CODE>-<A/B> | <placement>`, for example `GJ26 | I1-A | feed`.

## Platform limits

| Where | Limit | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| Meta advert main text | about 125 characters show before "See more"; the first 40 matter most on a phone | Put the offer or the hook in the first line |
| Meta advert headline | keep under about 40 characters | One idea |
| Instagram caption | first 125 characters show | Hook first; no clickable links, say "Link in bio" |
| TikTok caption | first 100 characters show | Direct and native; "Link in bio" |
| Email subject | under 50 characters (about 35 show on a phone) | Punchy and specific |
| Email preview text | under 90 characters | Extends the subject, never repeats it |
| WhatsApp message | under 80 words | One action, with exactly where to tap |
