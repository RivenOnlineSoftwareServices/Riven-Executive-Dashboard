# Glowming brand kit 2026: essentials for adverts and posts

The 2026 Glowming look is **warm, photography-led, structured and
restrained**. Cream `#DAC287` is the brand's warmth; Pomegranate red
`#BF2E48` is its signature moment. This file covers what an advert or social
post needs. Values here are the brand standard: use them exactly, never
approximate them, and never invent new ones.

## 1. Where it applies

- Adverts, social posts, sharing cards: **light by default**.
- A **dark hero version** is allowed when the brief earns it (a flavour-drama
  or campaign-hero moment). Name the reason. In a set, dark posts are the
  accent; the feed should read light overall.
- Posts on a **photograph** follow the photo's own rules (white text with a
  soft shadow and a darkening gradient), whatever the mode.

### Flavour colours

| Flavour | Colour | Lifted (for dark grounds and photos) |
|---|---|---|

Flavour colours go on **saturated accents only**: the product itself, small

on a dark photo: use `#D4A574` there.

## 3. Type

- **Axiforma Black** for display headlines (roughly 44px and up on the web,
  78px and up on a 1080 post). **Always ALL CAPS**, tight tracking
  (about -0.035em to -0.05em), line spacing below 1.0. Never sentence case,
  never at small sizes, never italic.
- **Geist** for everything else: labels, supporting lines, prices, small
  print. Sentence case lives in Geist. Weights used: 400, 500, 600, 700,
  and 900 for bold sentence-case headings.
- The flavour word in a headline may be coloured; it is never italic.
- **Outlined headline** (white letters with a dark outline) is allowed on a
  **flavour or colour field only**, never over a photograph. Over a photo,
  readability comes from a darkening gradient plus one soft shadow.
- If the Axiforma Black font file is not available on the computer, say so.
  Do not swap in another heavy font on a final.

## 4. Shape, spacing, depth

- Corners: panels 28px, small labels 14px; one shape family per post.
  No fully round "pill" labels on posts.
- One line weight: 1.5px.
- **Products never float.** Every placed pouch or sachet gets two shadows: a
  soft dark oval directly under it (the contact shadow, the important one)
  plus a softer cast shadow down and slightly right.
- **No flat colour fills.** A colour background is built: a soft light patch
  in the upper third, a gentle darkening toward the bottom, and a slightly
  darker floor under the product.

## 5. Logo rules

Official files only, from **SA Operations > GSA All Assets > 01_Logo**:

| File | Use on |
|---|---|
| Glowming SA Logo - Pomegranate with Dropshadow | Light digital backgrounds (the default) |
| Glowming SA Logo - Pomegranate | Flat: print, PDF, cream backgrounds |
| Glowming SA Logo - White Colour Stars with Dropshadow | Dark backgrounds and photographs (digital) |
| Glowming SA Logo - White Colour Stars | Flat: print on dark, photo overlays |
| Glowming SA Logo - All White | Single-colour print or watermarks only, never the normal dark logo |

- **Size on posts and adverts: one third of the frame width (33.91%), never
  smaller.** On a 1080-wide frame that is about 366px wide. Size it by width.
- Clear space around it: at least the height of the "G".
- The three stars (gold `#84754E`, pink `#DA188D`, red `#BF2E48`) are part of
  the logo: never remove, recolour or reorder them.
- **Never** redraw the logo, type it out as text, recolour it, stretch it or
  add effects. If no official variant suits the background, change the
  background area, not the logo.

## 6. Product pictures

- Product pictures are **never generated or drawn**. They come from
  **SA Operations > GSA All Assets > 02_Products**.
- **Groups come only from 02_Products > All Products.** Fanned sachets:
  `Sachets x4 - Fanned.png` or `Sachets - Upright and Fanned.png` (the only
  two). Pouch groups: `Pouches Website Fanned.png` (Anton's usual four-pouch
  group), `GAS Pouches Fanned.png`, `GAS Pouches Straight Line.png`. Also
  `GSA 4 Sachets in Pomegranate Splash.png` and the small cold and hot cup
  sets. Use them as they are (scale or let an edge crop them). **Never build a
  group from single cut-outs.** If a file is too small for the size needed,
  ask.
- Single flavour shots (pouch front and back, sachet, glass, cup, splash)
  live in **02_Products > <Flavour>**.
- **Copy that says "daily" shows sachets**, not pouches: sachets are what
  people use every day; pouches are what they buy.
- **Let the product peek in from the edge,** usually the right, ideally
  bottom right, when the design still works.
- Real sizes (for believable scenes): pouch **15 x 22 cm** (the biggest thing
  on the counter), sachet **4 x 13 cm**, tall glass 6.5 x 16 cm, short glass
  8 x 10 cm, mug 8 x 9 cm. Glasses and mugs are house props and may appear
  in generated scenes at the correct scale; the pouch and sachet may not.
- Badges: **07_Badges**. Other folders: 09_Elements (design elements),
  10_Hero Images, 12_Design-Backgrounds, 13_Lifestyle_Photos (Glowming's own
  photos, good for backgrounds), 14_Glowming_Affiliates (real affiliate
  photos, cleared for use in posts).

## 7. Photography rules

- Warm, natural daylight; soft, real settings; the moment of use (a hand
  stirring a sachet, a glass on a sunny counter), not a stock-photo set-up.
- **Never:** cool-toned photos; sterile lab or pharmacy looks; watermarked
  stock; AI-generated people (faces, hands, bodies) in any picture or video.
- Real people only from real photographs: Glowming's own, affiliate photos,
  or the hosts' own material.
- A Cape Town photo that carries a Hello Cape Town photo credit keeps that
  credit readable (add a darkening gradient behind it if needed).

## 8. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the exact hex values above | Invent a colour or "nearly" match one |
| Axiforma Black in caps for headlines, Geist for the rest | Axiforma in sentence case or at small sizes |
| Official logo at one third of the frame width | A small logo, a recoloured logo, a typed-out name |
| Official product pictures and groups | AI-drawn pouches or home-made groups |
| One message, one product hero per post | Two claims or a feature list on one post |
| Built, lit backgrounds and grounded products | Flat fills and floating products |
| Quiet small-caps web address on the bottom line, if needed | A white web-address pill stamped on the design |
| AA contrast on every piece of text | Pale text on a busy photo without a darkening gradient |
| Light posts, with dark as an earned accent | A mostly dark feed |
